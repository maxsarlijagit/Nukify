# Logo Nukify 0.3

Método: herramienta integrada image_gen. Imagen nueva con transparencia, guardada en `assets/nukify-logo.png` e incluida en el ejecutable. No se usó API/CLI de generación.

Prompt:

> Use case: logo-brand. Create an original friendly app mascot icon for Nukify, an open source node-based film compositing desktop app. A charming tiny rounded amber-orange node creature, with two simple dark eyes and a happy small smile, a little teal connector dot above and below connected by short dark strokes, subtly suggesting a vertical node graph. Clean bold vector-like silhouette, flat polished colors, minimal details, friendly professional creative tool. Centered square composition with generous transparent margins. Must remain legible at 32px and look good on a charcoal UI. Actual transparent background. No text, no letters, no shadows outside the icon, no gradients, no resemblance to Blender or Foundry Nuke logos. One single icon, not a sheet.
